# Vallora United U19 wins tournament with crucial 3-2 victory over Northbridge FC U19 in title decider

In a highly competitive title decider at Hollowmere Arena, Vallora United U19 clinched the championship by defeating Northbridge FC U19 3-2. Entering the final matchday just one point behind the leaders, Vallora rose with the win to 12 points and the top of the standings, pushing Northbridge to second place with 10 points.

## How it unfolded

Northbridge FC U19 struck early with Mathis Byrne’s powerful right-footed goal in the 2nd minute (xG 0.198), taking the lead and gaining initial momentum. Despite this, Vallora United U19 registered higher field tilt (55.3%) across the match and an overall expected goals (xG) tally nearly double that of their opponents (2.351 vs 1.272), indicating a strong attacking presence.

Equalising in the 18th minute, Alvaro Nagy’s header (xG 0.65) marked his first tournament goal and shifted the match momentum. Vallora pressed on, with Iker Andreou scoring a right-foot strike at the 27th minute (xG 0.052), giving Vallora the lead for the first time.

The half-time drama continued as Northbridge’s Szymon Winther equalised in stoppage time (45+3’) with a well-placed header (xG 0.455), balancing the match just before the break.

After the interval, Vallora took the lead decisively through Iker Andreou’s penalty at the 49th minute (xG 0.76), his second goal of the match, which proved to be the tournament-winning strike.

Northbridge increased their pressing intensity, accumulating 189 pressures during the match compared to Vallora’s 160, but did not score after the break. Vallora maintained a higher pass accuracy (79.57% vs 72.47%) and won a majority of duels (54% vs 45%), underpinning their overall edge.

The match was mostly in a balanced but intense regime, with several momentum swings. The control index hovered near neutral, reflecting a tight contest. Vallora used effective progressive passing and high involvement from attacking midfielder Iker Andreou, who had made eight touches in the box by 41' to sustain offensive pressure.

In the final stages, Northbridge mounted another pressing surge but lacked quality chances to force a draw. Vallora held territorial advantage and composure, securing the crucial points that crowned them champions.
