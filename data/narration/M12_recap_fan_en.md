# Vallora United U19 Win the Tournament by Beating Northbridge FC U19 in Title-Deciding Match

## How It Unfolded

Northbridge FC increased their pressing intensity in search of a response, but Vallora maintained their composure and held on to the victory that sealed their tournament triumph.
